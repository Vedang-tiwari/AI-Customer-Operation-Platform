# Organization Refund and Billing Dispute Policy

## 1. Overview
This policy governs all subscription billing, invoice adjustments, and refund requests across all customer tiers.

## 2. Refund Eligibility Window
Customers are eligible for a full 100% refund on subscription purchases if the request is submitted within 30 days of the transaction date. Requests made after 30 days are subject to proration or credit towards future service at the sole discretion of our billing management team.

## 3. Duplicate Charges
If a customer is inadvertently billed twice due to network retries, payment gateway errors, or duplicate subscriptions, our operations team will immediately verify the transaction logs and issue an automated refund for the duplicate charge within 1 to 2 business days.

## 4. Cancellation and Chargebacks
Subscriptions can be canceled at any time from the account billing portal. If a chargeback is initiated through a banking institution, the customer's organization account will temporarily enter a frozen review state until our finance department reconciles the disputed transaction.
