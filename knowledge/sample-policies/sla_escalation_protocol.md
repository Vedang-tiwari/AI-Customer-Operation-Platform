# Service Level Agreement (SLA) & Incident Escalation Protocol

## 1. SLA Response Time Targets
- **Severity 1 (Urgent):** System down, security breach, data corruption. Initial response time target is under 15 minutes. 24x7 coverage.
- **Severity 2 (High):** Major functionality impaired, billing discrepancy exceeding $500, customer blocked. Initial response target is under 1 hour.
- **Severity 3 (Normal):** General bugs, non-blocking inquiries. Initial response target is under 4 business hours.
- **Severity 4 (Low):** Feature requests, general questions. Initial response target is within 1 business day.

## 2. Escalation Workflow
When a ticket involves security compromise, unauthorized data disclosure, or payment gateway blockage, the deterministic routing engine automatically escalates the ticket to Urgent priority and reassigns it to the Security & Tier 2 Engineering queue.

## 3. High Frustration Handling
Tickets flagged with negative or frustrated sentiment are given high priority routing to senior support agents to ensure rapid empathetic resolution.
